# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AI Desk to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone — regardless of experience
level, background, identity, or ability. Many people who use and shape AI Desk are non-technical, and
that is exactly who it is for.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Using warm, plain, inclusive language.
- Being patient with questions, especially from non-technical users.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what's best for the people using AI Desk.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Dismissive or condescending responses to beginners.

## Scope & enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the
project publicly. To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately. Maintainers will review and
respond fairly and confidentially, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this
code.
