# Glossary (plain language)

If a user runs into a word they don't know, explain it like this.

- **AI Desk** — this whole workspace of AI helpers you talk to.
- **Assistant / agent** — a helper that takes on a task and uses judgment (e.g. drafting an email).
- **Quick tool / skill** — a small helper that does one job the same way each time (e.g. proofread).
- **Command** — a shortcut word you type, like `init` or `help`.
- **Profile** — what AI Desk remembers about you: your name, tone, business, and rules.
- **Onboarding** — the short setup chat you do by typing `init`.
- **Workspace** — the `workspace/` folder where your drafts and finished files are saved.
- **Connection / MCP** — an optional link to a real service (Gmail, Slack, calendar, etc.) that lets
  a helper read or act on live data. Fully optional.
- **Draft** — a version prepared for your review. AI Desk always drafts before doing anything
  outward.
- **Guardrails / boundaries** — your rules for what AI Desk must never do without asking.
- **Autonomy** — how much freedom you give AI Desk to act on its own vs. check with you first.
- **Catalog** — the list of assistants and tools you currently have.
- **Checkup** — a self-maintenance pass that keeps everything healthy and consistent.
- **Claude Code** — the app this runs inside; it's what reads your messages and does the work.
